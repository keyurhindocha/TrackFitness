Tips for finding a lost Fitbit
  1. Turn Bluetooth off on your other phones/tablets (or force-close the Fitbit app). A Fitbit that is connected to your phone usually stops advertising, so scanners can’t see it.
  2. Fitbits show up with names like Charge 6, Inspire 3, Versa 4, Luxe, Sense. Some show no name — look for a device whose signal changes as you move.
  3. Signal values: −40 is right next to you, −60 same room, −80 far / behind walls, −95 barely there.
  4. Hold the phone still for a second or two at each spot — readings jump around. Sweep slowly room by room and follow the “Warmer” arrows.
  5. Your body blocks Bluetooth. Turn in a slow circle; the strongest direction is roughly where it is.
  6. Check couch cushions, laundry, car seats, gym bag pockets, under the bed.
  7. If the battery is dead it won’t appear at all. In the Fitbit app, check “last synced” time to narrow down where it was.
